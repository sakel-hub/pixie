An enchanted glowing fairy companion for **Luanti** that accompanies you on all your travels! The mystical pixie illuminates pitch-black caverns with a warm personal light, sniffs out hidden ore veins deep underground, alerts you when monsters are creeping close, heals your wounds during perilous battles, and even makes the ultimate sacrifice as a Guardian Angel to rescue you from fatal blows.

## Gameplay & Highlights

- **Hands-Free Living Lantern**: A personal sphere of warm light glides beside you wherever you go—no more placing endless torches in caves or nighttime hikes!
- **Underground Ore Sniffer**: Your fairy tunes into deep mineral vibrations, sending sparkling starlight trails through rock walls to lead you straight to diamonds, mese, quartz, and gold.
- **Danger Early-Warning System**: Senses approaching monsters before you see them, sounding warning chimes and pointing light trails directly toward the threat.
- **Battle-Smart Companion**: Automatically flutters up and behind your shoulder during combat, letting your arrows, swords, and tools pass cleanly through without getting in your way.
- **Guardian Angel Rescue**: Sacrifices itself in a burst of celestial stardust to save your life when you take a fatal hit, giving you a second chance at survival.
- **Emergency Healing Pulses**: Gently swoops down to channel restorative healing sparks whenever you take damage on your adventures.
- **Loyalty That Grows**: Your bond deepens the longer you travel and survive together, unlocking powerful perks across 3 loyalty tiers.
- **8 Radiant Colors**: Discover wild pixies in eight glowing crystal colors dancing among wildflower fields and forest canopies.
- **Full of Personality**: Watch your companion perform joyous somersaults, aerial loops, barrel rolls, and celebrate whenever you strike rare treasure.
- **The Fairy Whistle**: Craft an enchanted flute to call your companion from across the world, check their distance, or command acrobatic tricks on cue.

## Finding & Befriending Your Pixie

### Where Wild Pixies Live
Wild pixies are peaceful nature spirits that flutter gently among lush tree canopies, wildflower clearings, deep forests, and crystalline grasslands. 

- **Attracting a Pixie**: Pixies love sweet blossoms and nectar! Hold any wild flower or flora in your hand and stand nearby. An intrigued wild pixie will notice your blossom, turn toward you, and hover playfully right in front of you with happy chime sounds.
- **8 Radiant Crystal Colors**:
  - **Starlight Azure** — Luminous celestial blue
  - **Sunbeam Amber** — Warm golden honey glow
  - **Arcane Violet** — Deep mystical amethyst purple
  - **Verdant Emerald** — Lush forest leaf green
  - **Glacial Frost** — Crisp winter ice azure
  - **Ember Ruby** — Fiery sunset crimson
  - **Astral Indigo** — Silvery twilight shine
  - **Mese Yellow** — Vibrant electric yellow

### Forming the Magical Bond
To tame a wild pixie, approach it while holding any flower, honey (`farming:honey`), or mese crystal fragment (`default:mese_crystal_fragment`) and **Right-Click** the pixie. 

A shower of colorful fairy fireworks and musical chimes will celebrate your new friendship! From that moment on, the pixie becomes your faithful companion, matching your travel speed across the world.

> **One Companion at a Time**: The magic of the fairy bond is deeply personal. A traveler may bond with one pixie at a time. If you choose to tame a new wild pixie, your previous companion will peacefully bid you farewell and return to the forest.

## Growing Together: Loyalty Tiers

Companions are living friends whose magic matures the longer you explore and survive together. As your bond strengthens over time, your pixie unlocks new abilities:

| Tier | Travel Time | Unlocked Abilities |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Fledgling** | 0 – 20 mins | **Living Lantern**: Warm personal lighting that follows you anywhere.<br>**Companion Modes**: Smooth flight following, shoulder perching, and stationary guard mode. |
| **Tier 2: Loyal Companion** | 20 – 60 mins | **Danger Radar**: Alerts you to hostile monsters within 14 blocks with chimes and light trails.<br>**Healing Pulses**: Periodically heals you (+2 HP every 8 seconds) when injured. |
| **Tier 3: Ancient Guardian** | 60+ mins | **Ore Sniffer**: Tracks underground diamonds, mese, quartz, and gold through solid rock.<br>**Guardian Angel**: Sacrifices itself to save you from fatal defeat. |

Reaching a new tier triggers celebratory chimes, stardust fireworks, and a congratulatory message in chat!

## Features & Magical Abilities

### 1. Hands-Free Living Lantern
Your pixie radiates a steady, cozy field of warm light (light level 13). Unlike static wall torches, this luminous aura glides smoothly alongside you as you walk, run, swim, or mine. When you step out into bright midday sun, the light automatically rests to conserve energy.

### 2. Underground Ore Sniffer (Tier 3+)
Deep underground, ancient guardian pixies can feel the resonance of buried minerals. While spelunking through caves and mine shafts, your companion will periodically scout nearby rock walls:
- Detects high-value veins including **diamonds, mese, quartz, gold, emeralds, and ancient debris**.
- Projects a sparkling starlight guidance trail that streams directly through stone toward the richest deposit.
- Celebrates big discoveries with excited chimes, 360° loops, and pirouettes!

### 3. Monster Danger Radar (Tier 2+)
Never get ambushed by surprise again! When dangerous predators or monsters creep within 14 blocks of you:
- Your pixie sounds an urgent warning chime.
- Projects a bright starlight beam pointing straight at the threat.
- Sends an early warning in chat so you can draw your weapon or take cover.

### 4. Battle-Smart Companion
During combat, your companion stays completely clear of your swings:
- **High Perch Stance**: When a fight begins, your pixie flies up and hovers safely above your rear shoulder, out of enemy melee reach.
- **Never Blocks Your Attacks**: Your companion's presence won't interrupt your sword swings, pickaxe strikes, or bow shots—attacks pass right through towards the enemy without accidental friendly fire.
- **Acrobatic Dodge**: If a stray monster attack comes too close, your pixie has an **85% chance to acrobatically roll and dodge** out of harm's way!

### 5. Guardian Angel: Sacrificial Rescue (Tier 3+)
If disaster strikes and you take what would be a fatal blow:
- Your faithful pixie intercepts the lethal strike in a blinding shockwave of celestial starlight and chimes!
- Your life is saved, immediately restoring you to **+5 HP** so you can escape or finish the fight.
- The pixie dissolves as a heroic sacrifice. While you survive your mistake, your friend is gone—encouraging you to protect your companion and venture out into enchanted groves to bond with a new wild spirit.

### 6. Soothing Healing Sparks (Tier 2+)
When exploring dangerous dungeons or recovering from a fall:
- Whenever your health is low (HP < 20), your companion periodically swoops over your chest with comforting chimes and healing sparkles, restoring **+2 HP every 8 seconds**.

### 7. Playful Tricks & Acrobatics
Pixies have joyful, mischievous personalities and love showing off in the air!
- **Aerial Maneuvers**: While cruising through the world, pixies spontaneously perform loop-the-loops, barrel rolls, and joyful backflips.
- **Resting & Frolicking**: When you pause near flowers, your companion flutters over to dance and inspect the petals.
- **Cheering Your Discoveries**: Striking diamond or mese veins prompts ecstatic celebratory somersaults.
- **Mirroring Your Jumps**: Jumping near your companion encourages them to mirror your leap with an enthusiastic flip!
- **Treats & Petting**: Offer any flower, honey, or mese fragment to restore **10 HP** to your friend, accompanied by cheerful heart sparkles and a happy somersault!

## Companion Commands & The Fairy Whistle

### Follow & Guard Modes
With an empty hand, **Right-Click** your pixie to toggle its stance:
- **Following (Default)**: Your companion flutters gracefully ahead, steering where you look to light your upcoming path. If you travel very fast or teleport, it swiftly catches up to your side.
- **Guarding / Waiting**: The pixie stays in place, hovering serenely in a glowing circular orbit. Use this to place your companion as a cozy living lantern at your home cottage, garden patio, or mine entrance.

### The Enchanted Fairy Whistle
Craft a musical **Fairy Whistle** (`pixie:whistle`) to stay connected across any distance:
- **Recall from Guard Mode**: **Right-Click** with the whistle to play a melodic flute tune. Your guarding companion hears your call across the entire world and instantly flies to your side with celebratory fireworks!
- **Perform Tricks**: Right-clicking while your buddy is already following orders them to perform an acrobatic trick on command.
- **Check Location & Distance**: **Shift + Right-Click** with the whistle (or type `/pixie status` in chat) to see your companion's current health, loyalty tier, and exact coordinates.
- **Crafting Recipe**:
  - `1x Any Flower / Flora` + `1x Gold Ingot` (or `Mese Crystal Fragment`) + `1x Stick`
- **Durability**: The enchanted whistle provides **30 uses** before its crystalline magic dissolves back into fairy dust.

### Chat Commands
You can also manage your companion from anywhere using chat:

| Command | What It Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| `/pixie status` | Displays companion name, health, loyalty tier, journey time, and guard coordinates. |
| `/pixie rename <Name>` | Gives your companion a custom name (e.g., `/pixie rename Navi`). |
| `/pixie nametag [show\|hide\|toggle]` | Toggles or customizes the in-world floating nametag above your companion. |
| `/pixie release` | Gently releases your companion back into the wild so you can tame another. |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How do I heal my pixie if it takes damage?**  
A: Pixies naturally recover health over time (+1 HP every 2 seconds). To heal your companion instantly, hold any wild flower, honey, or mese crystal fragment and **Right-Click** your companion to restore **10 HP** with cheerful heart sparkles.

**Q: What happens when I log off or the server restarts?**  
A: Your companion is safely preserved with your character profile. When you log back in, your pixie automatically materializes beside you with its name, loyalty progress, and health fully intact.

**Q: Can other players attack, steal, or rename my pixie?**  
A: No. Bonded pixies are attuned exclusively to their owner. Other players cannot command, rename, hurt, or un-tame your companion.

**Q: Can I change my companion's name later?**  
A: Yes! Simply type `/pixie rename <NewName>` at any time.

## Game & Mod Compatibility

The pixie companion works out of the box with standard Luanti games and automatically enhances its abilities when popular mods are present:

- **Voxelibre**: Detects ancient debris, deepslate ores, emeralds, and hostile creatures (creepers, wardens, wither skeletons).
- **Everness**: Spawns in crystal grasslands and detects multi-hued crystal ores and flora.
- **Ethereal NG**: Spawns in enchanted groves and detects illumishroom ores and fire blossoms.
- **Farming & x_farming**: Accepts sunflowers, roses, and custom crops as treats.
- **Mob Mods**: Full monster early-warning support for *x_mobs*, *Mobs Redo*, *Animalia*, *Creatura*, *Dmobs*, *Draconis*, *NSSM*, and *Pale Watcher*.

## Configuration & Settings

You can customize the mod's balance in your world settings (`settingtypes.txt`):

| Setting | Default | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| `pixie_show_nametags` | `true` | Show or hide floating name tags above tamed pixie companions. |
| `pixie_light_natural_threshold` | `12` | Sunlight level above which the personal lantern turns off to save energy. |
| `pixie_ore_max_y` | `64` | Maximum height for underground ore detection (only sniffs out ores below this level). |
| `pixie_loyalty_tier2_time` | `1200` | Journey seconds (20 mins) needed to reach Tier 2 (Loyal Companion). |
| `pixie_loyalty_tier3_time` | `3600` | Journey seconds (60 mins) needed to reach Tier 3 (Ancient Guardian). |
| `pixie_whistle_durability` | `30` | Number of times the Fairy Whistle can be used before dissolving into fairy dust. |
| `pixie_combat_retreat_duration` | `8.0` | Time (in seconds) the companion stays safely perched behind your shoulder after a battle. |
| `pixie_spawn_chance` | `4200` | Chance for a wild pixie to spawn during exploration (lower = more common, higher = rarer). |
| `pixie_spawn_active_max` | `2` | Maximum number of active pixies allowed within a 48-block radius. |
| `pixie_spawn_min_elevation` | `1` | Minimum elevation height for wild pixie spawning (1 = surface and above). |
| `pixie_enable_drops` | `true` | Whether wild pixies drop floral traces and glowing stardust when defeated. |
